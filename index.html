What is Studio Vedana?

We are an independent game development studio dedicated to pushing the boundaries of interactive storytelling. Rooted in the stark contrasts of modern noir, we craft experiences that linger in the shadows of the human psyche.

Our mechanics are surgical. Our narratives, relentless. Welcome to the edit suite of reality.
