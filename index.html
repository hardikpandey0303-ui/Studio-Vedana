What is
Studio Vedana?

We are an independent game development studio dedicated to pushing the boundaries of interactive storytelling. Rooted in the stark contrasts of modern noir, we craft experiences that linger in the shadows of the human psyche.

Our mechanics are surgical. Our narratives, relentless.

Surgical Mechanics

Choice-and-consequence systems where investigation and moral decisions carry lasting weight.

Psychological Noir

Atmospheric worldbuilding exploring tension, ambiguity, and human vulnerability across South Asian urban landscapes.
